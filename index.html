01 / About

The full life cycle, not just the pipeline

My work covers collection, ingestion, storage, processing and visualization: database and ETL development, dimensional modeling, report development, and big data engineering. I have migrated legacy Informatica and SSIS workloads onto Databricks and AWS Glue, turned nightly batch into Structured Streaming, and automated the whole path to production through Jenkins, GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD and Terraform.

The part I care most about is the unglamorous half: schema enforcement on streaming ingest, watermarking, reusable tokenization and masking modules, lineage you can actually hand to an auditor. A pipeline that moves data quickly but cannot explain itself is not finished.
